⚡ Bring-up Studio
Every function a pad can carry, read from the SoC pin header, and whether your board already uses that pad.

How to use this

  1. Pick a device on the right, or add one with ＋ (opens the guided wizard on the home page).
  2. The guide shows every bring-up layer. The current step is the first one that isn't green.
  3. Open a step: read why, click the file link, make the change in your editor, press Check.
  4. After deploying to the board, press Rescan board so board-side checks update.

Left side: what exists (live board, source tree, pins). Right side: what to do next.
