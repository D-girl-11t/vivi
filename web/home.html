    Rules vs. AI

    The guide is rule-based on purpose

    Every step comes from templates in knowledge/ and every ✓ comes from a real check (your source, your kernel tree, your board), so it can't make up a property or a pin that doesn't exist. It works offline, gives the same answer every time, and you can open the YAML to see why each step exists.
